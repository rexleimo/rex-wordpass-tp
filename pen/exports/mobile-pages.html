01 THE EMPTY WALLET MYTH
Most crypto product onboarding still assumes the user arrives with a funded wallet, a preferred chain and a mental model of gas. That is false for the majority of first-time sessions we instrumented.
We redesigned the first surface as a state machine: no wallet, wrong chain, empty balance, funded. Each state has its own CTA, helper copy and exit path.
“If the first screen cannot name the user’s current state, the product is lying.”
— MIRA CHEN
02 NINE-MINUTE TUNNEL
Median time from land to first successful message dropped from 27 minutes to 9. The biggest win was not fewer steps — it was fewer ambiguous ones.
